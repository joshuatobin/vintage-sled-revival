# Paint research · October 19, 2026

This is a first pass through relevant joined Facebook groups, not an exhaustive search. The app's nine starter references live in `dist/paint-data.js`. Their year ranges are source claims; none is a verified paint choice for the two garage projects. Browsing this library never changes saved findings or purchase gates.

## Sources inspected

- [1963–1965 Ski-Doos: 1964 yellow, red and gray discussion](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2987668478014245/posts/29069326732755063/) — private group, membership required. Read the visible replies and the original photographed labels. The MOTObase LV label reads `S2-0212.0`; a second photo shows Painter's Touch 2X Gloss Marigold. Both are approximate matches in the discussion. Members also suggest comparing protected original paint and consulting the Bombardier museum. Gray remains unresolved in this pass.
- [Vintage Skidoo Olympics: public 1964 color discussion](https://www.facebook.com/groups/778144523424225/posts/1783561909549143/) — public group. A [1968 reply](https://www.facebook.com/groups/778144523424225/posts/1783561909549143/?comment_id=1783606642878003) identifies Axalta Imron 3.5 HG Plus `RH-CG498 E`; the reply does not supply its mixing formula or specify the component/model. A [different reply](https://www.facebook.com/groups/778144523424225/posts/1783561909549143/?comment_id=1783640022874665) describes 1964 yellow as darker than 1965–1968. This is a disagreement to investigate, not a resolved year boundary.
- [Vintage Skidoo Olympics: 1970 yellow question](https://www.facebook.com/groups/778144523424225/posts/1784033102835357/) — visible replies did not establish another dependable formula in this pass.
- Vintage and Classic Ski-doo Restorations and Parts Swap 60s 70s 80s 90s — membership confirmed. The first group search surfaced Marketplace listings rather than useful code evidence; no formula is attributed to those results.
- [Oldski-doosleds paint reference](https://www.teshio.com/oldski-doosleds/paint.html) — linked from the early-year discussion. Read in the browser. Secondary collection of historical information and restorer reports, not an authoritative compatibility table. Four starter leads are transcribed from it: red `79668`, yellow `99-59033`, yellow `DU1372` Group `.01`, and black `84976`. Each retains its stated scope and unverified status.
- [Archived Bombardier chart, PDF page 4](https://www.teshio.com/oldski-doosleds/pdf_files/SKI-DOO%20paint%20codes%201959%20to%201991.pdf#page=4) — read the original scan in the browser. Page 4 distinguishes 1960–1963 yellow from a 1964–1970 yellow recipe. Some year annotations on the scan are handwritten. These are historical TE-series recipes, not current supplier codes. The later range conflicts with the community observation above.

## Publication and evidence rules

Store concise technical summaries, identifiers and clean source links. Keep private-source access labels visible. Do not republish member photographs, full posts, profile details or contact information. The app links to original photos for label review.

Do not infer an exact screen color from a photograph. The preview's yellow, red and black chips are illustrative color families. Do not convert historical cumulative mixing figures into a modern recipe without supplier verification. Do not imply current availability, compatibility or a confirmed chassis year.

Next research targets: original year-specific factory documentation, gray finish identification, the 1964 shade disagreement, and supplier confirmation of the exact product systems. No messages have been sent to group members.
