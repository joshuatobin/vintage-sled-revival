# Illustrated yearbook research

Current range: 1959–1979. Each year names one representative model reference, not the full lineup. These entries are independent of Joshua's project identities and must never confirm the early sled's year.

## Model identities

[Bombardier specification booklet](https://www.trailz.net/pub/Snowmobiles/SKIDOO/1996-1997/1997/MSPE_A97.PDF), original model identification index:

- PDF page 18: 1960–1969; names and model codes for K60/K61/K62, R6/AR6, BR64 and the later Olympiques.
- Page 17: 1970–1971.
- Page 16: 1972.
- Page 15: 1973–1976.
- Page 14: 1977–1979.

The [museum's first sales register description](https://museebombardier.com/en/artefacts/ski-doo-sales-register/) supports the 1959 starting point. Its [technical publications index](https://museebombardier.com/services/) provides original year-specific brochures. The 1960, 1963 and 1968 brochures were inspected visually to inform the early model-family schematics. The original schematic graphics were rejected and removed.

## Paint references retained

[Archived paint document](https://www.teshio.com/oldski-doosleds/pdf_files/SKI-DOO%20paint%20codes%201959%20to%201991.pdf), PDF page 4: historical R.M. recipes for 1959, 1960–1963, 1964–1970 and 1971–1979. Some range annotations are handwritten. These ranges do not establish an exact hood finish for the representative model. The 1964 disagreement from the existing paint library remains linked and unresolved.

The UI’s hex colors are illustrative choices, not measured readings or conversions from the formulas. For 1971–1979, the general yellow recipe must not be described as a proven hood finish for the selected Olympique.

## Revised scope and images

Joshua limited the range to 1959–1979 and rejected the schematic artwork. All sled drawings and infographic downloads have been removed; no 1980s/1990s model entries remain. The period brochures remain source references, not republished images. No replacement photograph is assigned to a year without evidence for that year/model. The owner’s original photos remain in their existing project records.

Selecting a year or decade changes only interface state; neither the shared garage nor browser recovery records is written. These references must never confirm the early sled’s chassis year.
